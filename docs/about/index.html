About

TaxSaveUSA Trending publishes short, plain-English briefs on what is trending in the US: news, tech, sports, entertainment, money and taxes.

Every brief is written in our own words from publicly reported information, names its source and links to it. We skip tragedies, graphic content and campaign politics. If a story changes, we update or remove the brief.

Nothing here is financial, legal or tax advice. For your own situation, talk to a qualified professional.
